What we run

Three jobs, done properly

Most small businesses have an email list sitting in a POS system doing nothing. We turn it into a channel you own — no algorithm, no ad spend, no rented audience.

Automated flows

Welcome series, abandoned checkout, post-purchase, and 90-day win-backs. Written once, earning every day after.

Monthly campaigns

Two to four sends a month with your offers, seasons, and news. You approve a calendar; we write, build, and send it.

List growth & deliverability

Capture forms at checkout and on your site, clean lists, and the DNS setup that keeps you out of spam.

How it works

Four weeks from first call to first send

The order matters here — each step depends on the one before it.

WEEK 01

Audit

We look at your list, your POS, and what you've sent before. You get the findings whether or not we work together.

WEEK 02

Set up

Platform, domain authentication, capture forms, and a clean import. This is the part that decides if you land in inboxes.

WEEK 03

Build flows

We write and build your core automations, in your voice, and you approve every email before it goes live.

WEEK 04

Send & report

First campaign goes out. From then on you get a monthly one-pager: what sent, what opened, what it made.
